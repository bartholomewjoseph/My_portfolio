Supporting Microplanning with GIS & Spatial Data

Using GIS and spatial data analysis to improve healthcare coverage and access to health facilities in Kaduna State, Nigeria. Evaluated settlements within a 5km buffer distance of health facilities in Takai and Sumalia LGAs, identifying population clusters lacking coverage to prioritize locations for new health facilities or temporary health posts. Designed 10 detailed ward maps for Kaura LGA using Atlas to support microplanning initiatives.
